Tired of your icons jumping to random positions every time you rotate your phone?

Every other launcher reflows your grid when the orientation changes — your carefully placed icons shuffle to new spots, your muscle memory is broken, and you're back to searching for everything.

Anchor Launcher is different. Icons stay anchored to the same physical screen location whether you're holding your phone in portrait or landscape. Rotate freely. Nothing moves.

<b>How it works</b>

When you rotate, Anchor mathematically transposes your icon grid so each icon lands at the same physical pixel it occupied before. The system rotation makes everything right-side-up as usual — the unique part is that your icons are still exactly where you left them.

No other Android launcher does this. It's been requested on XDA and GitHub for over a decade. Anchor ships it.

<b>Built for people who rotate</b>

— Tablet users who switch between portrait and landscape constantly
— Gaming phone and landscape-first device users
— Physical keyboard phone users (Fxtec, etc.)
— Anyone who has ever muttered "why did everything move again?"

<b>What else is included</b>

— 2D navigation matrix: organise your home screens into rows. Swipe left/right between pages in a row; swipe down/up to move between rows. Each row remembers its own scroll position independently.
— Alphabetical app drawer with A–Z letter index strip for fast one-tap access to any section
— Built on Lawnchair 15 — all the polish of a mature launcher with Anchor's spatial layer on top
— Clean, uncluttered home screen by default
— No ads. Ever.
