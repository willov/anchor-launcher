Bug fixes and improvements.

• Fixed icons and labels being clipped inside their cells on some screen sizes and grid configurations.
• Fixed a bug where swiping down from the top of the screen while on the top row would navigate down instead of playing the spring bounce.
• Fixed the workspace snapping one page to the right after dragging an icon on the upper rows.
• Fixed cross-row drags landing on the wrong page after switching rows mid-drag.
• New option in App Drawer → Advanced to disable alphabetical section dividers.
