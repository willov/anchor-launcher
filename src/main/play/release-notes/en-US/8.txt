Per-row home pages.

• Each row of home screens can now have its own starting page. Long-press the home screen and choose "Set as default page" to set it for the row you are on.
• Rows you haven't set one for are unchanged — they still start at their leftmost page.
• The wallpaper now rests centred on your home page, so the picture is framed the way you expect and pans either side of it as you swipe.
